# Security and Privacy

## Biometric data minimization

- The HRMS **does not store fingerprint templates, face templates or biometric images**. They stay
  on the devices. The ZKTeco handshake asks for attendance logs only; Hikvision names, card numbers
  and pictures in event records are discarded.
- The HRMS stores identity **references**: device user ID per device (`biometric_enrollments`), an
  optional card number (encrypted), the enrollment status/history and who enrolled.
- Raw event payloads are reduced to identifiers and codes and stored encrypted.
- Biometric identifiers are not shown on employee self-service pages; enrollment pages require
  `attendance.enrollment.view`.

## Secrets

| Secret | Storage | Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Device username/password | Laravel encryption (`APP_KEY`) | Hidden from serialization; never in props, logs, exceptions, exports; "blank keeps current" on edit |
| Hikvision push token | SHA-256 hash only | Shown once after creation |
| Connector secret | Laravel encryption | Shown once after creation/rotation |
| Card numbers | Laravel encryption | Never sent to pages |

Errors stored or shown (`last_error`, sync history) are generated by the adapters and contain no
credentials or URLs with credentials. Audit logs record "[redacted]" for hidden attributes.

## Authorization

Permissions (no role names in code): `attendance.view`, `attendance.scope.all`, `attendance.create`,
`attendance.adjust`, `attendance.approve_adjustment`, `attendance.process`, `attendance.finalize`,
`attendance.export`, `attendance.config.manage`, `attendance.roster.manage`,
`attendance.device.view|manage|configure|sync`, `attendance.log.view|import`,
`attendance.enrollment.view|manage`. Without `attendance.scope.all`, a user sees only direct reports
(`supervisor_id`) and themselves. Adjustments are decided by someone other than the requester
(`AttendanceAdjustmentPolicy`). Mass assignment is limited by `$fillable` and FormRequests; route
model binding plus scope checks prevent insecure direct object references; all queries use bindings.

## Integrity and audit

Raw events are append-only (model guard); corrections are adjustments with original and corrected
values; decided adjustments, audit logs and consumed payroll summaries cannot change. Device,
credential, token, enrollment, adjustment, policy, roster and summary actions are in
`attendance_audit_logs`.

## Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

Biometric attendance data is personal data under RA 10173. The National Privacy Commission states it
has **not yet issued guidelines specific to biometric processing**, which remains governed by the
DPA (NPC Advisory Opinion 2025-007,
https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NPC-Advisory-Opinion-No.-2025-007_Redacted.pdf).
NPC Advisory Opinion 2023-010 notes employees must be aware of the nature, purpose and extent of
workplace processing (https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Advisory-Opinion-No.-2023-010.pdf).
Whether biometric attendance data is *sensitive* personal information was not confirmed from an
official source in this session. The design therefore applies the stricter controls above.

Customer obligations (not enforceable by software alone; confirm with the customer's DPO/counsel):
privacy notice to employees, documented lawful basis and proportionality, privacy impact assessment,
retention schedule, data-sharing agreements for any hosted deployment, breach procedures.

## Items for the organization's Data Protection Officer

This document makes no legal conclusion. The DPO (or counsel) should confirm:

1. Lawful basis and proportionality for biometric attendance, and whether a non-biometric
   alternative (card, PIN, manual) must be offered.
2. Whether attendance and device-mapping data is treated as sensitive personal information.
3. The privacy notice given to employees (what is collected, where templates stay - on the device -
   who sees attendance, retention).
4. Retention periods for raw events, calculated days, adjustments, device sync history, audit logs
   and biometric mappings. (Removing the old test data does not replace a production retention policy.)
5. Whether a privacy impact assessment is required and done.
6. For hosted deployments and connectors: data-sharing / outsourcing agreements and cross-border
   transfer, if any.
7. Who may hold `attendance.scope.all`, `attendance.log.view` and `attendance.enrollment.view`.
8. Breach handling for device credentials (rotate passwords, push tokens and connector secrets).

## Access to sensitive attendance data (implemented)

| Data | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Raw events | `attendance.log.view` (Raw Logs page) or `report.attendance` (Attendance Log report), limited to `AttendanceScope` (everyone with `attendance.scope.all`, otherwise direct reports and self). Unmapped device users only with `attendance.scope.all`. Payloads are never shown. |
| Own attendance | `attendance.view_own` (+ `attendance.export_own` for CSV), own employee record only. |
| Biometric mappings | `attendance.enrollment.view` / `.manage`. Card numbers are encrypted and never sent to pages or reports. |
| Device credentials, push tokens, connector secrets | Never returned to pages, logs or exceptions; set by `attendance.device.configure`; tokens and secrets shown once at creation/rotation. |
| Audit trail | `attendance_audit_logs`, append-only; written for device, connector, enrollment, adjustment, policy, roster and summary actions. |
| Organization isolation | A connector tied to a legal entity or location may only deliver for devices of that entity/location (`AttendanceDeviceService::assertConnectorScope`); connector requests are HMAC-signed with replay protection; a connector can submit only for devices assigned to it. The HRMS is single-organization per database; legal entities are the isolation unit. |

## Retention

**No retention or purge mechanism exists.** No retention period is invented here. Recommended
implementation once the DPO approves a schedule: a `retention_policies` record per legal entity and
data class (raw events, days, adjustments, sync runs, audit logs, mappings), a dry-run-first
`attendance:purge` command that deletes only records older than the approved period, never touches
data referenced by finalized or consumed payroll (`attendance_period_summaries.consumed_run_id`,
locked days) or under legal hold, writes an audit record of what was purged, and is scheduled only
after explicit approval.
